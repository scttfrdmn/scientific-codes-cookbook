Two independent codebases with different search heuristics landing on the same optimum is
a statement about the NUMERICS that no single-tool check can make. The tolerance is set by
IQ-TREE's printed precision -- it reports 3 decimals, so +-0.0005 of rounding is inherent --
not by how close the two happen to land. Asserting 1e-6 would be asserting a print format.
