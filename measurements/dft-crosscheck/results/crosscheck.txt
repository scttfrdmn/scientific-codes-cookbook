tolerance_Ang     0.0300  want difference < this

Total energies are NOT compared: an LCAO pseudopotential total energy and a PAW
plane-wave total energy have different zeros, so only a structural observable like the
equilibrium lattice constant is comparable at all. The XC functional (LDA) and the
k-mesh (6x6x6) are matched, and the same quadratic fit is applied to both scans, so the
remaining difference is the basis treatment -- which is what sets the tolerance.
